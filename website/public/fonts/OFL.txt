The subsets were taken from the Fontsource distributions of the upstream
releases (@fontsource/spectral, @fontsource-variable/source-sans-3 5.3.0;
@fontsource-variable/jetbrains-mono, added with the docs site) and vendored
rather than pulled as dependencies: the site needs exactly three files, and
vendoring keeps them out of the npm dependency graph and out of Vite's asset
pipeline.
