Composer keyboard probe — round 5

Five rounds have cleared everything about how the composer is described: attributes, one-line geometry, placeholder, task title, field count, and being wrapped in a form. No replica has ever reproduced the bug. What no replica has ever copied is what the composer does while you type.

collecting…
The claim being tested. The real composer is a React controlled component. On every keystroke it reassigns the textarea's value and mutates style.height to autosize, and it re-renders again whenever a transcript event arrives. iOS keeps a pending autocorrection on the marked word; rewriting .value mid-word is a known way to discard it. Every probe field so far has been inert HTML, which is exactly why they all came back clean.

Prediction, before you run it: D (value reassigned on every keystroke) and G (all three behaviours at once) fail to autocorrect. A, E and F are fine. If D and G autocorrect normally, this theory is wrong too and I'll say so.
